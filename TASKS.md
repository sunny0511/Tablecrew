**Reconciliation note, added 2026-08.** This list describes the order features should be considered *launch-ready* to end users — a product-completeness axis. It is genuinely different from, and no longer matches, `docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`'s *engineering build* order (the F-milestone sequence) as of the Discover re-scope: that plan now builds Discover (F7) before full Crew management/Chat/Rating and Notifications (F8/F9), while this list still places Crew management (5) and Notifications (7) ahead of Discover (8). Not reconciling these into one ordering was a deliberate choice, not an oversight — forcing them to match would mean either delaying Discover's build for no real dependency reason, or reordering this list in a way that misrepresents what "launch-ready" actually requires.
