# Series A Technical & Product Diligence Review

**Reviewers:** acting as VP of Product (Airbnb) and Principal Engineer (Google), engaged to do adversarial diligence on the TableCrew knowledge base ahead of a Series A. **Date:** 2026-08. **Method:** every document in `docs/` plus the root files was read in full, either directly or by a dedicated reviewer assigned to a domain (product, architecture/data/API, security/privacy/engineering-process, design/brand/copy, GTM/investor), each instructed to find real weaknesses and fix them directly in the documents, not merely list them. This document is the compiled record of what was found and what was changed, organized by the sixteen review categories requested: product vision, UX, business model, competition, scalability, engineering, security, database, APIs, analytics, monetization, trust & safety, legal, privacy, accessibility, internationalization.

This review also had to absorb three significant pieces of live direction from the founder mid-review: (1) TableCrew is a **platform**, not narrowly "a mobile app" — the mobile app is its primary current surface, not its definition; (2) the Phase 0 anchor city is **Hyderabad, India** — this is an India-first company, not a U.S.-first one with a later India expansion; (3) Table headcount is **not** a single rigid number — it's a 2–8 hard range with activity-dependent recommended sizes. All three are folded into the findings below because they materially changed what "consistent" means across the doc set.

## 1. The single highest-priority finding: the India-first pivot is not yet fully re-threaded

`docs/ROADMAP.md` and `docs/LEGAL.md` (§11, newly added) now correctly state that Hyderabad, India is the Phase 0 home market, and `docs/TASKS.md` tracks this. **Why it matters more than any other single finding:** almost every other document in this repository — `docs/SECURITY.md`'s assumption that Persona covers the launch market, `docs/API_SPEC.md`'s Stripe-centric, implicitly card-first payment design (India's dominant everyday payment rail is UPI, not cards), `docs/INVESTOR_OVERVIEW.md`'s TAM/SAM/SOM and illustrative unit economics, and `docs/MARKETING.md`'s channel assumptions — was originally reasoned from a U.S.-first starting point. Reflagging the anchor city without re-deriving those numbers and vendor assumptions would leave a diligence-ready-looking document set that is quietly wrong about its own home market. **What was done:** `docs/ROADMAP.md`, `docs/LEGAL.md`, `docs/MARKETING.md`, and `docs/TASKS.md` were updated with the corrected framing and explicit follow-up items (Persona's Aadhaar coverage, UPI payment support, India-specific entity structure, IT Rules 2021 intermediary obligations in place of a U.S.-centric Section 230/DSA framing as the first gating regime). **What was not done, and is now the top item in `docs/TASKS.md`:** a full re-derivation of `docs/INVESTOR_OVERVIEW.md`'s unit economics and TAM for an India-first model, and a payments-rail redesign in `docs/API_SPEC.md`/`docs/FIREBASE.md` for UPI. These are large enough that doing them silently, inside an already-large review pass, risked getting the numbers wrong under time pressure rather than flagging them clearly for a dedicated follow-up pass — that judgment call is itself worth surfacing to whoever reads this document.

## 2. Product vision, UX, and product risk (owned review: `PRD.md`, `USER_RESEARCH.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `FEATURES.md`, `SUCCESS_METRICS.md`, `USER_PERSONAS.md`)

- **Missing feature, now fixed:** nothing specified what happens during an actual safety emergency at a Table. Added FR-D12–14 to `PRD.md` (in-app duress signal, locale-aware emergency number, opt-in trusted-contact location share) with a matching mechanism added to `SECURITY.md`.
- **Missing requirement, now fixed:** the 18+ age floor (see Section 7 below) was a legal recommendation with no functional requirement behind it. Added as NFR-15/16 in `PRD.md` and a `FEATURES.md` row.
- **Missing edge case, now fixed:** a Table could silently empty out between the T-2h viability check and start time with no re-check (FR-T14a added); a Confirmed Table had no cancellation path for a venue closing or a host emergency (FR-T9a, FR-T14b added); bill-split disputes/refunds had no defined flow (FR-T25a added); post-Table ratings had no defense against retaliatory or gamed reviews (FR-T29a: simultaneous reveal).
- **Placeholder violation, now fixed:** `VALUES.md`'s Featured Venue cap was described as "a defined share" with no actual number — a bare `FEATURES.md` violation of this repository's own "no TBD" standard. Fixed to a stated 30% cap.
- **Gap, now fixed:** `SUCCESS_METRICS.md` had no subscription conversion/churn/win-back metrics despite `ROADMAP.md` referencing a "Phase 1 conversion target" that didn't exist anywhere. Added §3.6.
- Numeric cross-check across `USER_RESEARCH.md`/`USER_PERSONAS.md`/`PRD.md` found no drift in sample sizes or persona details, but did catch a genuine floor-vs-target ambiguity between `SUCCESS_METRICS.md`'s 99.3% crash-free guardrail and `ROADMAP.md`/`PRD.md`'s 99.5% launch bar — clarified as intentional (floor vs. target), not a silent contradiction.

## 3. Architecture, database, APIs, scalability, and analytics (owned review: `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `DATABASE.md`, `API_SPEC.md`, `FIREBASE.md`)

- **Technical inaccuracy, now fixed:** `ARCHITECTURE.md` conflated Firestore's genuine multi-region replication guarantee with true active-active regional failover — Cloud Functions deploy to a single region, so most write paths (including payment and capacity-critical ones) do not actually survive a regional Cloud Functions outage even though Firestore's data plane might. Corrected with real, stated RPO/RTO figures instead of an implied "we're multi-region so we're covered" claim.
- **Missing mechanism, now fixed:** no mutating endpoint had idempotency keys, meaning a retried `requestSeat` or `createSplitRequest` on a flaky mobile connection could double-book a Table or double-charge a card. Added mandatory idempotency keys and a backing `idempotencyKeys` collection.
- **Security/privacy bug, now fixed:** `DATABASE.md`'s original flat `users/{userId}` document with a blanket `allow read: if isSignedIn()` rule would have exposed every user's `trustSignals`, `blockedUserIds`, and verification detail to every other signed-in user — Firestore rules can't redact individual fields, only whole documents, so this directly broke `SECURITY.md`'s "blocking is silent" guarantee. Fixed by splitting into a public document and a `private/profile` subcollection with field-appropriate rules.
- **Missing schema, now fixed:** `splitRequests/{splitRequestId}` was referenced throughout `API_SPEC.md` but was never actually defined in `DATABASE.md`.
- **Legal/technical contradiction, now fixed:** the database schema didn't distinguish "delete on request" (personal data) from "retain, anonymized, for ~7 years" (the financial ledger), contradicting the tax/financial-recordkeeping reality named in `VALUES.md`'s amended language (Section 8 below).
- **Resilience gaps, now fixed:** no documented behavior for a Typesense Cloud outage (Discover would have simply gone down; added a Firestore-backed degraded-mode fallback), and rate limiting was per-account only (added a secondary App-Check/device-keyed limit against multi-account abuse).
- **Analytics gap, now fixed:** metrics in `SUCCESS_METRICS.md` were described in prose with no canonical event-naming taxonomy anywhere; added a concrete event table in `FIREBASE.md` (`table_created`, `rsvp_confirmed`, `table_no_show`, `report_filed`, etc.) so every KPI is actually computable, not just implied.

## 4. Security, trust & safety, privacy, and engineering process (owned review: `SECURITY.md`, `ENGINEERING_GUIDELINES.md`, `TESTING.md`, `DEPLOYMENT.md`, `CI_CD.md`)

- **Missing capability, now fixed:** the in-Table emergency/duress response mechanism (locale-aware emergency number, quick-exit signal, opt-in location share) referenced in Section 2 above was built out in `SECURITY.md` as the technical/process source of truth.
- **Legal exposure, now fixed:** `SECURITY.md`'s language didn't clearly distinguish "we verify identity" (what Persona actually does) from "we run a criminal background check" (a materially different, FCRA/ban-the-box-regulated claim) — see `LEGAL.md` §5. Corrected to be precise.
- **Weak enforcement, now fixed:** the 18+ age floor had no stated enforcement mechanism beyond an implicit assumption; self-reported age is trivially fakeable. Added a signup-time DOB gate plus an ID-derived age cross-check gating Discover access, with the Crew-only tier's residual risk stated explicitly rather than ignored.
- **Abuse vector, now fixed:** no-show accountability had no defense against a spiteful or coordinated false mark; added single-source weighting and pattern-detection on statistical outliers.
- **Missing process, now fixed:** no distinct escalation path existed for off-platform stalking/harassment following a Discover match, a materially higher physical-safety case than an ordinary in-app report; added a dedicated reason code and severity classification.
- **Operational risk, now fixed:** the single Trust & Safety review desk (named as a scaling concern in `FEATURES.md`) had no surge plan for a report-volume spike outpacing hiring; added a concrete surge protocol to `SECURITY.md`.
- **Testing gap, now fixed:** no chaos/game-day testing existed for report-queue floods, Stripe webhook outages, or regional degradation; added a quarterly game-day practice to `TESTING.md`.
- **Deployment gap, now fixed:** no safeguard existed against deploying to the wrong regional Firebase project for a data-localization jurisdiction; added a deploy-time region assertion to `CI_CD.md`/`DEPLOYMENT.md`.

## 5. Design, brand, copy, accessibility, and internationalization (owned review: `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `BRAND_GUIDELINES.md`, `COPY_GUIDELINES.md`)

- **Unenforceable promise, now fixed:** `VALUES.md`'s "always labeled 'Sponsored'" commitment for Featured Venue placements had zero corresponding spec in `COPY_GUIDELINES.md`. Added exact label copy, placement rule, and a `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` visual-treatment cross-reference.
- **Legal exposure, now fixed:** nothing prevented future copy or marketing imagery from gamifying alcohol (a "drinks" badge, bar-crawl framing), which `LEGAL.md` §4 flags as real liability exposure beyond a neutral "dinner, drinks optional" framing. Added explicit guardrails to `COPY_GUIDELINES.md` and `BRAND_GUIDELINES.md`.
- **Inclusion gap, now fixed:** nothing addressed physical venue accessibility (step-free entry, accessible restrooms) — an app can be WCAG-compliant and still be unusable in practice for a wheelchair user if the venue itself isn't accessible. Added a requirement to `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, flagged for a `DATABASE.md`/`PRD.md` schema follow-up (a `Venue.accessibilityInfo`-style field and Discover filter — not yet built, tracked in `TASKS.md`).
- **Aspirational-only requirements, now made concrete:** right-to-left layout support and age-diverse marketing imagery were previously stated as values-level aspirations with no enforceable spec; both now have concrete mechanisms in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and `BRAND_GUIDELINES.md` respectively.
- **Cultural rigidity, now fixed:** bill-splitting copy guidance implicitly assumed a Western even-split norm; loosened to require culturally-neutral, host-choice-reflecting language.

## 6. Business model, competition, monetization, and regulatory/platform risk (owned review: `MARKETING.md`, `SEO.md`, `INVESTOR_OVERVIEW.md`)

- **Named-risk gap, now fixed:** `INVESTOR_OVERVIEW.md`'s risk section had no mention of U.S. state "dating service"/"social referral service" statutes that may require background-check disclosures or specific cancellation rights and could apply to Discover — its absence was itself a diligence red flag for a stranger-matching product, given `LEGAL.md` §2 flags this as a real Phase 1 gate.
- **Missing unit economics, now fixed:** no CAC/LTV/payback modeling existed anywhere alongside the TAM/SAM/SOM walkthrough. Added an explicitly-illustrative estimate with stated assumptions.
- **Missing named risks, now fixed:** app store distribution/policy risk, payment processor underwriting risk (Stripe and similar processors have historically scrutinized "stranger meetup" marketplaces), and marketplace disintermediation (users taking a good match off-platform) were all absent and are now named with mitigations.
- **Overclean presentation, now fixed:** venue partnership revenue and competitive-response risk were both presented too cleanly; added realism (fragmented, slow-sales-cycle restaurant industry; honest fast-follow-competition analysis grounded in the density/liquidity moat argument from `VISION.md`, explicitly conceding the software feature set is clonable).
- **Internal contradiction, now fixed:** `MARKETING.md`'s referral mechanic offered "priority Discover placement" as an earned/paid perk, which read as paid-for-priority in the exact surface `VALUES.md`'s Featured Venue carve-out says must never be paid-for-priority. Reframed as a non-ranking-affecting "Featured Host" badge.

## 7. Foundation-document contradictions found and fixed directly (not delegated)

- **Real contradiction:** `VISION.md` originally said Crews ship "Year 2–3," directly contradicting `ROADMAP.md`, where Crews are the entire point of Phase 0 (Year 1). Fixed by deriving `VISION.md`'s milestone timeline directly from `ROADMAP.md`'s phase months instead of restating it independently, so the two documents can't drift apart again.
- **Real, named tension (not swept under the rug):** `MISSION.md`'s "anyone, anywhere" promise is in genuine tension with `SECURITY.md`'s government-ID-plus-liveness requirement for Discover, which structurally excludes people without formal ID (undocumented immigrants, refugees, some unbanked populations). `MISSION.md` now states this plainly, scopes the promise honestly (Crew-first is open to anyone with a phone number; Discover's promise is bounded by verification-vendor market coverage), and tracks a non-document verification alternative as an open item rather than pretending the tension doesn't exist.
- **Real tension, now reconciled:** `VALUES.md`'s "ad-model monetization is largely off the table" claim sat uneasily next to `PRODUCT.md`'s "Featured Venue" placement product. `VALUES.md` now draws the actual, enforceable line: sponsored venue placement is allowed, must be labeled, capped, and can never touch Discover's person-to-person matching — a real behavioral rule, not a semantic dodge.
- **Real conflict, now bounded:** `VALUES.md`'s "real, working deletion" promise didn't acknowledge that financial/tax law requires retaining transaction records for ~7 years. Now states the exception precisely: personal data deletes/anonymizes immediately, a minimal anonymized financial ledger is retained as legally required.
- **Missing document, now created:** there was no `LEGAL.md` anywhere in the original repository, despite the product verifying government ID, matching strangers for in-person meetings, processing cross-border payments, and operating in multiple legal regimes. `docs/LEGAL.md` now covers entity structure, ToS/insurance gating, dating-service-statute risk, host/venue liability, alcohol liability, background-check-vs-identity-verification precision, content-moderation liability (Section 230/DSA/India's IT Rules), multi-regime data protection, the 18+ age-floor recommendation, arbitration, IP/trademark clearance, and the India-first re-scoping in §11.

## What's still open

Tracked in `docs/TASKS.md`: the India-first unit-economics and payments-rail re-derivation (Section 1 above); Persona's Aadhaar/India-ID coverage confirmation; an Indian entity-structure decision with counsel; a `Venue.accessibilityInfo` schema addition; confirming the Mixpanel + Firebase Analytics dual-tool split is intentional rather than redundant spend. None of these were silently dropped — each has an owner-shaped next action in `TASKS.md`, consistent with this repository's standing rule against silent gaps.
